Civil Rights Intake Questionnaire

 

Individuals who believe they are victims of discrimination should submit a Civil Rights Intake Questionnaire to the Arizona Attorney General's Office.

After you complete this questionnaire you will be contacted to schedule an intake interview with this office. If you prefer to submit by mail, please download and print this form for submission.
